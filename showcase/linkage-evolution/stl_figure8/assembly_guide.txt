4-BAR LINKAGE ASSEMBLY GUIDE
========================================

Evolved Linkage Parameters:
  L1 (Ground):   4.480 units = 89.6 mm
  L2 (Crank):    1.230 units = 24.6 mm
  L3 (Coupler):  4.310 units = 86.2 mm
  L4 (Follower): 2.780 units = 55.6 mm
  Tracer Point:  (2.618, 1.500) on coupler

Parts List:
  - ground_plate.stl (1x) - Base with fixed pivot mounts
  - crank.stl (1x) - Input link, attach to pivot A
  - coupler.stl (1x) - With tracer point marker
  - follower.stl (1x) - Attach to pivot D

Hardware Needed:
  - 4x M5 bolts (for pivots)
  - 4x M5 nuts
  - 8x M5 washers
  - Optional: bearings for smoother motion

Assembly:
  1. Mount ground plate to a stable surface
  2. Attach crank to pivot A (left fixed pivot)
  3. Attach follower to pivot D (right fixed pivot)
  4. Connect coupler between crank and follower
  5. The tracer point marker shows where to attach a pen

Operation:
  - Rotate the crank (L2) to trace the evolved curve
  - Attach a pen to the tracer point for drawing
